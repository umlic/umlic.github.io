RESEARCH OVERVIEW

Study coordinated attacks on cooperating agents and learn team policies that can withstand them.

The Wolfpack attack targets an initial agent and the teammates that assist it. Wolfpack-Adversarial Learning for MARL (WALL) trains policies against this coordinated disruption to improve robustness across the team.
