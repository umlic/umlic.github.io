FoX: Formation-aware exploration in multi-agent reinforcement learning

Yonghyeon Jo · Sunwoo Lee · Junghyuk Yeom · Seungyul Han

UNIST · Machine Learning & Intelligent Control Lab

Read the paper ↗

RESEARCH OVERVIEW

Guide exploration through diverse team formations in partially observable cooperative tasks.

FoX organizes exploration around formations to reduce redundant search. Agents learn to recognize their formation from local observations and explore meaningful alternatives, with evaluations in sparse SMAC and Google Research Football tasks.
